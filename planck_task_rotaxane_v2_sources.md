## 2. Concept grounding

The prompt requires no obscure knowledge. Every principle used in the golden solution is established, pre-cutoff, textbook or review-level chemistry. Verified references:

**(a) Chirality of mechanically interlocked molecules; mechanical planar chirality; the requirement for an "orientated" macrocycle plus a directional axle.**
Jamieson, E. M. G.; Modicom, F.; Goldup, S. M. "Chirality in rotaxanes and catenanes." *Chem. Soc. Rev.* **2018**, *47*, 5266-5311. doi:10.1039/c8cs00097b
Bordoli, R. J.; Goldup, S. M. "An Efficient Approach to Mechanically Planar Chiral Rotaxanes." *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* **2014**, *136*, 4817-4820. doi:10.1021/ja412715m

**(b) Achirality conferred by a rotoreflection axis alone — the principle behind $M6$ (Steps 27-31).**
Standard stereochemistry: a molecule is achiral if and only if it possesses *some* improper operation ($\sigma$, $i$, or $S_n$); possession of an $S_n$ axis suffices even when no mirror plane and no inversion centre is present. The canonical textbook example is **1,3,5,7-tetrafluorocyclooctatetraene**, achiral by virtue of an $S_4$ axis despite having neither $\sigma$ nor $i$.
Eliel, E. L.; Wilen, S. H. *Stereochemistry of Organic Compounds*; Wiley: New York, 1994 — chapter on symmetry and chirality.

Note that $M6$ is a direct structural homologue of this textbook case: an even-membered ring whose residues are locked alternately above and below the mean plane, exactly the substitution pattern that generates $S_n$ achirality in the tub-shaped cyclooctatetraene.

**(c) Cyclo[n]aramides are real, and they really do form rotaxanes** — so the prompt's premise that all 30 rotaxanes are isolable is not contrived.
Wang, H.; et al.; Yuan, L. "Macrocyclic shape-persistency of cyclo[6]aramide results in enhanced multipoint recognition for the highly efficient template-directed synthesis of rotaxanes." *Chem. Sci.* **2017**, *8*, 1281. doi:10.1039/C6SC04714A
Review: "Hydrogen-bonded aromatic amide macrocycles: synthesis, properties and functions." *Org. Biomol. Chem.* **2022**. doi:10.1039/d2ob01263d

**(d) Meso relationships and the $(R,S)$ vs $(R,R)$ distinction; axial chirality of 1,1'-binaphthyls** (Steps 32-37) — standard undergraduate/graduate stereochemistry, no citation required.
